SNOW DURATION: MISSING versus ZERO FOR A SNOW-FREE YEAR
Period 1976-2025 (hydrological years 1977-2025), threshold 5 mm

Definition A (current) : a snow-free year has NO duration value.
Definition B (proposed): a snow-free year has duration 0.

235 selected stations, 11,276 station-years with at least 10 observations.

==============================================================================
4. READING THIS
==============================================================================

Observed median trend moves from -8.68 to -7.78 days per decade (+0.91)
when snow-free years are counted as zero rather than dropped.

Definition A answers: when a snow season occurred, how long was it?
Definition B answers: how long was the snow season, counting zero
                      when there was none?

Both are legitimate. B is the one that registers a station losing its
snow season entirely, which under A simply leaves the sample. For a
thesis about long-term snow change that disappearance is arguably the
change of interest, and it is currently recorded as missing data.

Whichever is chosen must be stated in the methods. Under A the number
of contributing stations varies year to year, and a reader will
otherwise assume it is constant.
